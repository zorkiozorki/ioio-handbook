## What's Inside the Breadboard Kit

The Breadboard Kit is intended for **second-year students in the TEI (Tangible and Embodied Interaction) course** and for **Master's students** working with physical interaction, prototyping, and embedded systems.

The kit includes:

- 1x [Arduino Nano 33 BLE Sense Rev2](https://docs.arduino.cc/hardware/nano-33-ble-sense-rev2/)
- 1x [Breadboard](https://www.seeedstudio.com/Breadboard-Clear-8-2-x-5-3cm-p-1108.html)
- 1x USB-A to Micro-USB cable
- 1x set of [Jumper Wires](https://www.seeedstudio.com/1-pin-dual-female-jumper-wire-100mm-50pcs-pack-p-260.html)
- 1x [NeoPixel Ring](https://www.adafruit.com/product/1643)
- 1x [Micro Servo Motor](https://www.seeedstudio.com/Grove-Servo-p-1241.html)
- [Potentiometers](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/Grove-Rotary_Angle_Sensor/)
- [Push Buttons](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/Grove-Button/)
- [Piezo Buzzer](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/Grove-Buzzer/)
- [Assorted LEDs](https://www.seeedstudio.com/LED-Pack-p-1502.html)

> **Important:** Standard LEDs are one of the more fragile components in the kit and can fail more often than the other parts. If an LED does not light up, try another LED and check its polarity before troubleshooting the Arduino or breadboard.
